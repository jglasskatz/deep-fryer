EJI technical documentation, pp. 93–94: E_NEHD is the sum of tract-specific extreme heat days over 2018–2022 divided by five; heat values were crosswalked from 2010 to 2020 tracts. Thirteen tracts nationally were interpolated; local interpolation status is not separately identified. The heat entry does not state the historical baseline years or seasonal window.

This summary describes exposure to locally unusual maximum temperatures. It is not an absolute-temperature or heat-index threshold, NWS HeatRisk, a count of simultaneous countywide events, or a future projection. Relative extreme-heat definitions differ from local Cal-Adapt definitions.

Scoring

No adopted or scientific safe annual-day target was established. Target, level_pct and scoring remain null. A percentile identifies unusually hot days; it does not define a safe annual number of such days.
