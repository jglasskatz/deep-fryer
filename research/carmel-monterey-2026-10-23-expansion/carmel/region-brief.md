# Carmel-by-the-Sea city-limits region brief

**Scope.** This portrait concerns the incorporated City of Carmel-by-the-Sea, California (Census place GEOID 0611250), rather than Carmel Valley or a wider “Carmel area.” The Census Bureau’s incorporated-place map identifies the city as an incorporated place in Monterey County; it also cautions that its mapped legal boundaries are for statistical purposes and are not a legal determination of jurisdiction. Use the City’s parcel/GIS tools and Monterey County LAFCO boundary materials when a jurisdictional boundary decision matters. ([Census Bureau incorporated-place map](https://www2.census.gov/geo/pvs/bas/bas24maps/st06_ca/incplace/p0611250_carmel-by-the-sea/BAS24P10600011250.pdf); [Census TIGERweb incorporated places table](https://tigerweb.geo.census.gov/tigerwebmain/Files/acs26/tigerweb_acs26_incplace_2025_acs25_ca.html); [City GIS mapping tool](https://ci.carmel.ca.us/post/gis-mapping-tool))

## Geography and boundaries

- Carmel-by-the-Sea is a Monterey County incorporated place. The Census Bureau’s 2023 Boundary and Annexation Survey map depicts the city’s outline, neighboring Monterey to the north and the Pacific/Carmel Bay coast to the west; it identifies Monterey County as the subject county. The map’s statistical-boundary disclaimer means the City/LAFCO boundary should govern any parcel-level inclusion question. ([Census Bureau map](https://www2.census.gov/geo/pvs/bas/bas24maps/st06_ca/incplace/p0611250_carmel-by-the-sea/BAS24P10600011250.pdf); [Monterey County LAFCO incorporated-city GIS layer](https://maps.co.monterey.ca.us/server/rest/services/LAFCO/Incorporated_Cities_2/FeatureServer/2))
- The City says its General Plan covers both the city and its “Sphere of Influence”; the General Plan is combined with the Local Coastal Program. Keep sphere-of-influence or coastal-planning coverage distinct from the incorporated city limits. ([City General Plan](https://ci.carmel.ca.us/post/general-plan); [City Local Coastal Program](https://ci.carmel.ca.us/post/local-coastal-program))
- The City states that Carmel-by-the-Sea lies entirely within the coastal zone. Its Local Coastal Program includes land-use, circulation, coastal-access/recreation, and coastal-resource-management elements. ([City Local Coastal Program](https://ci.carmel.ca.us/post/local-coastal-program))
- Several service geographies extend beyond city limits. Carmel Area Wastewater District (CAWD) includes the city plus outlying county areas such as Carmel Woods, Hatton Fields, parts of lower Carmel Valley, Carmel Meadows, Hacienda Carmel, Del Mesa Carmel and others; its stated service area is about 5.5 square miles and about 11,000 permanent residents. California American Water’s Monterey tariff area also covers multiple incorporated cities and unincorporated areas. Neither is a city-only statistical denominator. ([CAWD service-area description](https://www.cawd.org/service-area); [California American Water Monterey service area](https://www.amwater.com/caaw/Customer-Service-Billing/Rates/Monterey-District))

## Governance and public data owners

- The City Council adopts city policy, plans, budgets and ordinances; the City’s Community Planning and Building/Planning Division implements the General Plan and Local Coastal Program. The City publishes its General Plan and elements, including the 2023–2031 Housing Element (amended July 2026), on its website. ([City General Plan](https://ci.carmel.ca.us/post/general-plan); [City Planning Division](https://ci.carmel.ca.us/planning))
- The City’s budget page is a recurring source for annual budgets, audited annual comprehensive financial reports, quarterly sales-tax reports and fee schedules. ([City Budget and financial reports](https://ci.carmel.ca.us/budget))
- The City’s parcel/GIS mapping tool makes historic permit files and parcel information available. The city’s annual planning documents and adopted Climate Action and Climate Adaptation Plans are linked from the City’s long-range planning page. ([City GIS mapping tool](https://ci.carmel.ca.us/post/gis-mapping-tool); [City Long-Range Planning Initiatives](https://ci.carmel.ca.us/post/long-range-planning-initiatives))
- Monterey County LAFCO publishes municipal service reviews/sphere-of-influence studies and maps; its incorporated-city GIS layer labels Carmel-by-the-Sea. County GIS search results describe an open-data and maps portal, but the pages returned 403 when opened during this research, so they are not relied on here. ([Monterey County LAFCO GIS layer](https://maps.co.monterey.ca.us/server/rest/services/LAFCO/Incorporated_Cities_2/FeatureServer/2); [County GIS portal](https://www.countyofmonterey.gov/business/developing-here/gis-mapping-data), page access failed; [County LAFCO studies and maps](https://www.countyofmonterey.gov/government/government-links/lafco/studies-maps), page access failed)
- AMBAG is the federally designated Metropolitan Planning Organization and Council of Governments serving Monterey, San Benito and Santa Cruz counties. It prepares regional transportation planning and regional housing, population and employment forecasts; these are regional context and must not be treated as city-only observations. ([AMBAG About Us](https://www.ambag.org/about-us); [AMBAG planning](https://ambag.org/planning))
- Monterey Bay Air Resources District (MBARD) is responsible for air quality across Monterey, Santa Cruz and San Benito Counties and operates ambient-air monitoring across the North Central Coast Air Basin. Its monitoring/network geography is regional rather than coterminous with Carmel city limits. ([MBARD Air Quality and Planning](https://www.mbard.org/air-quality-and-planning))

## Institutions and service footprints

- **Utilities:** the City lists Pacific Gas & Electric (electricity/gas), California American Water (water), GreenWaste Recovery (solid waste/recycling), and CAWD (sewer) among local providers. CalAm and CAWD reporting geographies extend beyond Carmel city limits; match data to city parcels or city boundaries before using it as a city indicator. ([City Utilities](https://ci.carmel.ca.us/post/utilities); [CalAm service area](https://www.amwater.com/caaw/Customer-Service-Billing/Rates/Monterey-District); [CAWD service area](https://www.cawd.org/service-area))
- **Schools:** Carmel Unified School District explicitly spans Pebble Beach, Carmel-by-the-Sea, Carmel Valley and Big Sur. Its schools and district data therefore do not describe city residents alone without a city-specific allocation. ([CUSD Enrollment & Residency](https://www.carmelunified.org/departments/student-services/enrollment-residency))
- **Higher education:** Monterey Peninsula College says it serves Carmel and several other Monterey Peninsula and Big Sur communities, with multiple campuses. Its service population is regional. ([MPC About](https://mpc.edu/about/index.html))
- **Health care and safety:** The City’s Police & Fire page lists Community Hospital of the Monterey Peninsula as a local contact. The City Police Department information covers law enforcement within city limits; the Monterey Fire Department staffs the Carmel Fire Department under the City’s public-safety arrangement. ([City Police & Fire](https://ci.carmel.ca.us/police-fire))
- **Regional agencies:** AMBAG (MPO/COG), MBARD (air quality), Monterey County LAFCO, CalAm and CAWD publish material relevant to the city but use regional or district boundaries. ([AMBAG](https://www.ambag.org/about-us); [MBARD](https://www.mbard.org/air-quality-and-planning); [Monterey County LAFCO GIS layer](https://maps.co.monterey.ca.us/server/rest/services/LAFCO/Incorporated_Cities_2/FeatureServer/2); [CalAm](https://www.amwater.com/caaw/Customer-Service-Billing/Rates/Monterey-District); [CAWD](https://www.cawd.org/service-area))

## Statistical geographies and cautions

- The best standard ACS match is the **Carmel-by-the-Sea city** incorporated-place geography (GEOID **0611250**). The Census TIGERweb incorporated-place table identifies the exact place name and GEOID; Census’s map identifies it as an incorporated place in Monterey County. Use ACS place-level estimates where available, recording the ACS vintage and its margins of error. ([Census TIGERweb incorporated places table](https://tigerweb.geo.census.gov/tigerwebmain/Files/acs26/tigerweb_acs26_incplace_2025_acs25_ca.html); [Census Bureau boundary map](https://www2.census.gov/geo/pvs/bas/bas24maps/st06_ca/incplace/p0611250_carmel-by-the-sea/BAS24P10600011250.pdf))
- **County** estimates cover all of Monterey County and are not city substitutes. **Carmel Valley** is not part of this portrait’s named municipal geography; similarly named regional service areas (CAWD, CalAm, CUSD, MPC and MBARD) cover places beyond the city. ([Census Bureau map](https://www2.census.gov/geo/pvs/bas/bas24maps/st06_ca/incplace/p0611250_carmel-by-the-sea/BAS24P10600011250.pdf); [CAWD service area](https://www.cawd.org/service-area); [CalAm Monterey service area](https://www.amwater.com/caaw/Customer-Service-Billing/Rates/Monterey-District); [CUSD boundaries](https://www.carmelunified.org/departments/student-services/enrollment-residency); [MPC regional communities](https://mpc.edu/about/index.html); [MBARD region](https://www.mbard.org/air-quality-and-planning))
- A Carmel-by-the-Sea-specific **PUMA does not exist**: Census defines PUMAs as statistical areas containing at least 100,000 people, much larger than this city. PUMA estimates therefore cannot be read as city-only. ([Census PUMA overview](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/geography/guidance/geo-areas/pumas.html))
- Census tract or block-group data can be used only with an explicit spatial crosswalk/aggregation against the city boundary; partial tracts and vintage differences may create allocation error. The Census map itself is a dated statistical boundary representation and expressly disclaims jurisdictional authority. For parcel-level scoping, consult the City GIS or LAFCO’s current municipal map. ([Census boundary map](https://www2.census.gov/geo/pvs/bas/bas24maps/st06_ca/incplace/p0611250_carmel-by-the-sea/BAS24P10600011250.pdf); [City GIS](https://ci.carmel.ca.us/post/gis-mapping-tool); [Monterey County LAFCO GIS layer](https://maps.co.monterey.ca.us/server/rest/services/LAFCO/Incorporated_Cities_2/FeatureServer/2))
