# NYC and Santa Cruz policy evidence study — research draft

Pinned repository baseline: `d189a966649ffb4c876879a9bcd27f88b51f7809`.
Research as-of date: 2026-10-26. These records are retrospective policy evidence,
not prospective `PolicyImpact` records, portrait amendments or publication approvals.

The two region files retain full source-author research records. The independent
review describes its bounded factual coverage and limitations; it does not
establish every study's causal assumptions or verify every contextual sentence.
The user-facing derived view is `site/policy-view/`, built at `/policies/`.

Local slice mappings are conceptual relationships. They do not establish target
attainment, wedge movement, joint household wellbeing, or additive policy impact.
City, unincorporated county, water service area, transit district and regional
energy-provider footprints must not be collapsed into city-limit measurements.

Only the source exhibits actually present under `qa/evidence/` are locally archived.
The remaining sources have live links and retrieval/review notes, not a complete
immutable archive. Reproduction and publication review should acquire the missing
exhibits. No hash-bound human approval is claimed or requested by these files.

Validation: `node --test scripts/policy-study.test.mjs` checks the portable data,
slice identities, local links and preservation boundaries. This is not statistical
replication or the database-backed research-verify publication gate. See the task
validation report for exactly which repo/Keel checks ran and any blockers.
