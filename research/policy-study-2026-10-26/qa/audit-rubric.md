# Independent policy-claim audit rubric

## Gates
1. **Atomic verification:** independently open a primary source for every headline number and strongest claim; record URL, inspected passage/table, publication/as-of date and match result. A secondary locator is not primary verification.
2. **Population and jurisdiction:** label NYC city, Santa Cruz city, Santa Cruz county, California or multi-county service territory exactly; no transplanting a regional metric to a city.
3. **Measure:** distinguish expenditure, eligibility, households served, installations and compliance outputs from wellbeing/environmental outcomes. Record numerator/denominator, unit, period and population. Percentage change is not percentage-point change.
4. **Attribution:** separate randomized/quasi-experimental estimates with a counterfactual from uncontrolled before-after, monitored outcomes, modeled projections and policy intent. Preserve uncertainty and design limits; correlation and municipal attribution are not identification.
5. **Completeness:** report material null/adverse findings, enforcement or distributional tradeoffs, outcome reversals and implementation limitations; do not select only favorable time windows.
6. **Visual semantics:** evidence/design tier separate from verification status; no composite causal score, synthetic observed doughnut movement or use of existing projected_value fields for retrospective changes. Numeric values optional when unsupported.
7. **Provenance/reproducibility:** retain primary URLs, source dates, access date, calculation formulas and original units. Cite bounded claims rather than a broad source list.

## Review outcomes
- PASS: primary evidence independently supports bounded claim.
- CORRECT: specific wording, scope, unit or design correction needed before publication.
- UNVERIFIED: source unavailable or claim not yet checked; does not imply falsity.
- EXCLUDE: no defensible support or misleading claim not repairable within evidence.

No claims reviewed yet at rubric creation. Research coverage is purposive, not a systematic policy ranking or causal meta-analysis.
