# Independent adversarial review protocol

Prepared 2026-10-18 for Carmel-by-the-Sea **city limits** and **Monterey County** portraits. This protocol is a review method, not findings or authorization to publish. Review outputs and evidence stay in this research directory; no app edits or database writes.

## Claim-level checklist

1. **Claim identity and geography.** Freeze indicator slug, construct, value, unit, data year/period, source URL and claimed geography before checking. For Carmel, require the incorporated city, not Carmel Valley, a ZIP/ZCTA, postal “Carmel,” school district, water service area, peninsula, county or metro. For Monterey County, distinguish the county from Monterey city, a regional consortium or an agency service territory. A source can contain several correct values: compare the row for the claimed geography. Explicitly label permitted nonlocal contextual data; never relabel it as a city or county measurement. Apply the actual portrait policy and the user's narrower geographic scope before the repository defaults.
2. **Data year: 2021 or later.** Check measurement year separately from publication, download, dashboard update and report title dates. Identify ACS single-year versus five-year release and state the five-year period; an endpoint of 2021+ is not proof every observation is 2021+. Older baselines and forecasts are context or rejected candidates, not qualifying current measurements. Flag unavailable year, projections and postdated claims. Record whether a newer comparable compliant observation exists.
3. **Construct and universe.** Match the framework indicator definition, unit and population universe; reproduce the source's qualifications. Examples requiring special care: uninsured under 65 versus all residents; high-school attainment among adults 25+; registered-voter versus eligible-voter turnout; household broadband subscription versus availability; renter households versus people or all households; median low-income household energy burden versus an overall mean; territorial versus consumption emissions. A good source for a different construct does not verify the proposed indicator.
4. **Numerator, denominator and transformations.** Record exact table/variable/row and numerator/denominator where relevant. Recompute derived percentages, rates, complements and unit conversions; preserve legitimate source rounding. Match geography, period and statistical family, especially annual population in per-capita calculations. State exclusions, missing/zero-income households, suppressed cells and sampling uncertainty. Do not sum percentages, average municipal rates into a county value, treat missing releases as zero, or present administrative service-area totals as jurisdiction totals.
5. **Source tiers.** Audit the actual producer and methodology, not just the hostname or researcher label. Default tiers are local official government; state/federal statistical agencies; established research institutions; cited secondary reporting. Tier 2+ needs a documented higher-tier search and reason for fallback. Prefer primary, dated, stable, methodologically explicit material within a tier. Disqualify unreadable paywalls/snippets, ambiguous geography, unsupported press-release numbers and goals presented as observations. Record conflicting compliant sources and why one wins. A county source about Carmel is not automatically a city observation.
6. **Scoring.** Resolve the indicator's referenced threshold and any explicit authorized override with its rationale. Verify target, direction, units and scale before arithmetic. Higher-is-better: `(target − value) / scale × 100`; lower-is-better: `(value − target) / scale × 100`; default scale is `abs(target)` and zero targets require an explicit scale. For bands, use distance outside the band divided by band width; inside use negative distance to the nearest edge divided by width. Reproduce repository rounding to one decimal. Zero is boundary, positive is shortfall/overshoot, negative is safe. Keep `level_pct` separate from capped display `viz_level`; default dimension aggregation is worst-of. Never score an unverified value or a gap as measured success.
7. **Independent refetch AND archived exhibit.** Fetch the precise cited URL independently. Inspect the archived page/document as primary evidence, with the fresh source corroborating it. Require the actual value, year, unit, geography and table/figure/footnote in view. For PDFs, inspect the direct archived document and identify page/table; a screenshot of a landing page is insufficient. Check paths exist, timestamps, provenance and that the exhibit supports this claim, not another release. For derived values, archive all inputs and explain calculation. Search snippets, source homepages and plausible citations are not exhibits. Record source changes or discrepancies; distinguish live-fetch access failure from missing captured support.

## Verdicts and gaps

Execution clarification: the requested review JSON stores **source-content** support in `content_supported` and the separate archive result in `exhibit_checked`. A source-supported numeric claim can therefore have `content_supported: "pass"` with `exhibit_checked: false`; this is not overall publication acceptance. The session importer must force such a missing-exhibit claim to `uncertain`, as directed for this campaign. The overall acceptance standard below still requires both axes.

For each numeric claim, return an auditable row with claim identity, live-source result, exhibit result, semantic/policy/scoring issues, final `pass|fail|uncertain`, and a short exact reason with source/exhibit locator. `pass` requires observed support (including a reproducible derivation and stated rounding) for the correct construct, geography and period plus usable archived support. `fail` is a demonstrated contradiction, wrong construct/geography/year, unsupported claim in accessible material, or broken derivation. `uncertain` is a documented unresolved access, ambiguity or evidence problem; it must not be counted as verified. Report source-content support separately from overall policy acceptance when they differ.

Audit every gap for indicator coverage, dated search trail, attempted scales and tiers, and nearest rejected candidate with URL, year and rejection reason. “No data found” means no compliant data found in those searches, not proof no data exists. Test likely primary sources independently, especially where a regional source could be allowed by an explicit dimension policy. A truthful gap stays unscored and visible; do not fabricate completeness or fill it with a county proxy for Carmel.

## Repository limitations to compensate for during this review

- `packages/research/src/verify.ts` checks schema, URL response, calendar-year recency and scale enum. It does not establish semantic geography, construct, denominator, source tier, captured-file existence/content, scoring or nonfuture data year. Recency uses the runtime year minus the supplied year, so publication-year mistakes and future years need manual review. A successful HEAD/GET proves reachability only. Gaps pass URL/recency/scale vacuously, so manually audit them.
- `docs/strategy-findings.md` records earlier false negatives from comparing borough and city figures, and a correct value misattributed to its report publication year. It also identifies refetch-only uncertainty and self-reported tiers as weaknesses. Inspect captured evidence and tier trails independently; do not infer correctness from deterministic gate success, model choice or source reputation.
- The vendored researcher text requires evidence; the command-level fallback permits recording capture failure. Treat that exception as disclosed incomplete evidence, not a verified exhibit. Any schema-valid path can still point to missing or irrelevant material.
- No applicable `AGENTS.md` was found in this repository or its ancestor directories during the initial protocol read. Requested reviewer model identity is supplied by orchestration; actual token counts and USD cost are unavailable unless metering is explicitly exposed. Do not estimate or invent them.
