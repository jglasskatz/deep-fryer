Volunteering and Internships

Depending upon project needs, RCDMC cooperates with local colleges to host student internships (paid or unpaid) for experience and deeper learning in on-farm conservation science and projects. Community members simply wanting to contribute their time or skills to the RCD’s work are also welcome to inquire about options for volunteer opportunities for community event or in-field project assistance.

For more information about internship or volunteering opportunities, please contact our Project Administrator.
