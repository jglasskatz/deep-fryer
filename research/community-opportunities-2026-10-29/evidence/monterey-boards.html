Boards, Committees, and Commissions

The quality of life in the County of Monterey is greatly enhanced by numerous residents volunteering their time and energy to help the County of Monterey Board of Supervisors shape policies for current and future generations. Should you seek additional information to a specific Board, Commission or Committee (BCC), please scroll and select the BCC of your choice. Each BCC is staffed by a county representative or an outside agency who may provide you with additional information.
