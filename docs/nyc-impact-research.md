# NYC portrait family — impact research synthesis

*Compiled 2026-08-03 from two parallel research streams (administration/goals; borough data/precedents).
Input to Phase 1 of `docs/nyc-research-brief.md`: the proposed portrait config per tenant.*

Audience: Mayor Mamdani's office. Six portraits: `nyc` + five boroughs (already provisioned).

---

## 1. The strategic frame

**Don't lead with Raworth. Lead with the administration's own measure.**
No evidence Mamdani's team has ever referenced doughnut economics; searches surface only hostile
"Mamdani doesn't understand economics" op-eds. But in April 2026 he released the charter-mandated
**True Cost of Living (TCOL) measure** (Urban Institute-built, annual, 81% ballot approval in 2022):

- **62% of NYC residents (~5M people) below the true cost of living**
- 73% of children; **87% of Bronx children**
- Family threshold $159,197; average resource gap $39,603
- 44% white / 66% Black / 78% Hispanic / 63% Asian-PI below

That *is* a social foundation, already adopted, already his headline. The pitch:
**"TCOL is your social foundation. We add the ecological ceiling and the borough lens."**

**Anchor every threshold in adopted NYC law**, not imported ideology — the pre-emption of the
inevitable "doughnut = degrowth" attack (Cato, Fortune, Heritage are already running it against
his other policies). The portrait then reads as "tracking laws NYC already passed":
LL66 (80x50) · LL97 (building emissions) · Zero Waste Act 2023 · HealthyNYC/LL93 (life expectancy)
· LL148 (30% canopy by 2040) · LL195 (bus/bike lanes) · Vision Zero · Rikers closure law ·
NYS class-size law · charter §1151-c (TCOL itself).

**The borough cut is the differentiator.** Every major portrait (Amsterdam, Brussels, Melbourne)
admits metro averages hide inequality; none has done a rigorous five-way sub-city comparison.
Melbourne's own docs concede metro averages "dilute diverse experiences." Five doughnuts answer
the strongest published critique of the genre. Bonus: **NYC was a Thriving Cities Initiative
advisory city in 2019–20** (named in DEAL's methodology guide) — "NYC finally gets the portrait
it advised on."

**He likes this artifact class.** The 100-days site is an interactive, borough-sortable
accountability map; his quote: "this website lets people see exactly when and where we're
investing in our city." Third-party promise trackers (Mamdani Meter, mamdanitracker.nyc) already
exist — an official portrait lets City Hall own the scoreboard.

**Baseline everything to January 2026.** The wedges that look worst (shelter census 96,500 record,
right-to-counsel at 27.8%, Rikers ~6,700 vs 4,100 beds, zero-waste badly off-track) are inherited
conditions and targets in his own plans. Trajectory framing ("A Crisis Inherited, A Choice Ahead"
— the Coalition for the Homeless's own words) converts every bad wedge from indictment to mandate.

**Climate framing is affordability-first.** His climate politics are deliberately muted (Heatmap,
Inside Climate News both document this). Every ecological wedge routes through cost and health:
LL97 = lower bills · air = Bronx kids' asthma (his $20M congestion-pricing asthma investment) ·
heat = worker safety (his June 2026 EO — the one ecological topic he has personally
executive-ordered) · CSO = swimmable neighborhoods.

## 2. Headline numbers the portraits will surface (verified)

The five-doughnut contrast, one line each:

| Measure | Worst | Best | Spread |
|---|---|---|---|
| Life expectancy (NCHS 2020–22 — label vintage, COVID-depressed) | Bronx 76.7 | Manhattan 83.0 | 6.3 yr |
| Child poverty (ACS 2024 1-yr) | Bronx 39.0% | SI 11.6% | 3.4× |
| Median household income (ACS 2024) | Bronx $46,040 | Manhattan $106,403 | 2.3× |
| Severe rent burden ≥50% (ACS 2024) | Bronx 37.1% | Manhattan 24.2% | 1.5× |
| Child asthma ED visits /10k (2023) | Bronx 239.8 | SI 55.9 | 4.3× |
| Eviction filings /1k rental units (2024) | Bronx 104.1 | Manhattan 36.6 | 2.8× |
| Food insecurity (MMG 2025) | Bronx 22.6% | SI 12.5% | 1.8× |
| No home internet (ACS 2024) | Bronx 8.3% | Manhattan 3.3% | 2.5× |
| Tree canopy (2021 LiDAR) | Brooklyn/Queens 19.5% | SI 33.1% | 1.7× |
| Overdose deaths /100k (prov. 2024) | Bronx 60.1 | Manhattan 26.8 | 2.2× |
| Unemployment (June 2026) | Bronx 7.1% (worst of 62 NY counties) | — | — |

Neighborhood extremes for drilldowns: LE 90.3 (Sunnyside/Woodside) vs 75.0 (Brownsville);
asthma ~356/10k (East Harlem) vs 23.2 (South Beach–Tottenville).

**Four "surprise" contrasts that justify five doughnuts** (not just "Bronx worst"):

1. **Staten Island paradox** — most canopy, best AC access, but 50.8% drive alone
   (vs Manhattan 5.2%), longest commute (45.4 min), 2nd-highest obesity, 2nd-highest
   pregnancy-associated mortality (72.2/100k). High green, high carbon, mediocre health.
2. **Manhattan inversion** — highest income and raw PM2.5/NO2 (7.7 µg/m³, 18.6 ppb) but far
   from the worst asthma; East/Central Harlem carry near-Bronx outcomes inside the richest borough.
3. **Queens squeeze** — lowest official poverty (13.3%) but 2nd-highest under the cost-adjusted
   Poverty Tracker (23%); most foreign-born (47.5%); 9.8% overcrowding.
4. **Brooklyn spread** — 11.3 years of life expectancy between Brownsville and Fort Greene;
   largest absolute meal gap (85.9M meals).

**The analytically strongest point in the whole portrait:** exposure ≠ harm. Manhattan has the
highest measured PM2.5; the Bronx has 4.3× its child asthma ED rate. PM2.5-attributable child
asthma visits run ~4.5× higher in high-poverty neighborhoods (NYCCAS). Build wedges around
**exposure × vulnerability** — more rigorous, more persuasive, and the single best argument for
borough-level doughnuts. It is also the narrative for why the Bronx got the first municipal
grocery store (Hunts Point, end-2027) and the $20M asthma investment: **"the portrait shows why."**

**Anchor-fact hygiene** (verifier will care):
- "Bronx 62nd of 62 counties" is 2023-vintage; CHR dropped ordinal ranks in 2024.
  Current form: "worst national decile for Community Conditions (CHR 2025)."
- Borough LE (76.7 etc.) is 2020–22 NCHS; don't juxtapose with citywide 82.6 (2023) unlabeled.
- Life expectancy 83.2 (2024 prov.) already beats the HealthyNYC 83.0 target — and accrued under
  Adams, as did Vision Zero's record-low 205 deaths. Frame those wedges as "maintain + close the
  racial/borough gap," never as citywide victory laps.

## 3. Dimension → indicators, thresholds, and data (the config skeleton)

Format: framework dimension → indicator(s) | threshold anchor (adopted goal) | borough-level source.
★ = directly measures a Mamdani signature policy.

### Social foundation

- **food** — Food insecurity rate (MMG, county, annual); SNAP receipt (HRA borough, quarterly);
  grocery basket affordability ★ (municipal grocery 30% discount basket is a measurable price
  wedge; Hunts Point "77% struggle to afford basics" is the admin's own stat).
  Threshold: Food Forward NYC universal-access goal (2031).
- **health** — Life expectancy vs **HealthyNYC 83.0 + gap-narrowing** (LL93 of 2024); premature
  mortality (YPLL: Bronx 9,451 vs Manhattan 4,960); child asthma ED ★ (asthma investment).
  Source: DOHMH Community Health Profiles (59 CDs), EpiQuery, CHR.
- **education** — Class-size compliance % (NYS law: 100% by 2029-30, ~64% now); free 2-K/3-K seat
  coverage vs demand ★ (universal childcare, $1.7B); 4-yr graduation (DOE InfoHub borough tab);
  chronic absenteeism (InfoHub, not stale Open Data).
- **income-work** — **% below TCOL (the master indicator)** ★; unemployment (NYSDOL LAUS monthly);
  median wage vs $30x30 trajectory ★. Threshold: TCOL threshold itself (charter §1151-c).
- **water-sanitation** — Citywide drinking-water quality (flat by design — say so); **lead service
  lines by borough** (DEP dataset, semiannual — the differentiating indicator); water bill burden.
- **energy** — Energy cost burden (DOE LEAD, county/tract); Con Ed arrears/shutoffs; AC access
  (HVS 2023: Bronx 88.5% lowest) ★ (heat EO); UER county monthly consumption.
- **networks** — Broadband adoption (ACS S2801: Bronx 88.9% vs Manhattan 94.8%) ★ (Big Apple
  Connect 80% at NYCHA); bus speed ★ (his 6-minutes metric) + fare spend as % of TCOL transport
  budget ★ (registers fare-free buses when it lands, quantifies the gap while stalled);
  Fair Fares enrollment %.
- **housing** — Rent burden ≥30/≥50% (ACS/Furman); eviction filings per 1k units (OCA/Furman
  tracker) + right-to-counsel representation %; shelter census (DHS monthly — location ≠ origin,
  disclose); production vs **Block by Block 200k/200k** ★; rent freeze as threshold rationale ★.
  Gold standard cadence note: HVS is triennial (2023 latest).
- **gender-equality** — Earnings gap, LFP by sex (ACS); pregnancy-associated mortality (Bronx 84.9,
  SI 72.2, Black–white 5.3×) vs HealthyNYC maternal target; mothers' labor-force participation ★
  (childcare as gendered constraint). Honest flag: weakest borough dimension, proxies only.
- **social-equity** — TCOL by race (his own headline: 44% vs 78%); Gini (ACS B19083); Racial
  Equity Plan indicators (charter-mandated, his office publishes) ★; NYCHA repair times
  ($5.6B/5yr vs $78B need — show slope, not level).
- **political-voice** — Turnout by borough (CFB dataset, registered-voter denominator — disclose
  vs CVAP); participatory budgeting reach; language access. Keep light: thin official-target
  territory.
- **peace-justice** — Major felonies (Tisch quarterly briefings — use their numbers) ★; jail
  population vs 4,100-bed glidepath (show trajectory, not binary "closed") — most politically
  radioactive wedge; % mental-health calls handled by non-police response ★ (Community Safety
  office KPI); Vision Zero deaths (record-low 205 in 2025, credit-attribution care).
  Gap: incarceration by borough of residence not published.

### Ecological ceiling — three-tier scale policy

**Tier 1 (borough-comparable, measured):**
- **air-pollution** — NYCCAS PM2.5/NO2 (borough-native, best-in-class); pair concentration with
  attributable asthma burden (exposure × vulnerability) ★ (congestion pricing −22% PM2.5 in zone;
  South Bronx caveat honestly noted). Threshold: WHO guideline + NYCCAS trend.
- **land-conversion** — Tree canopy vs **30% by 2040 (LL148/Urban Forest Plan)**: SI 33.1 →
  Brooklyn/Queens 19.5; impervious surface (2017 raster, per-borough geodatabases); park access
  (Walk-to-a-Park 84.2% vs 85% goal — pick one definition, disclose). Careful: parks-budget wedge
  currently indicts his own 0.5% vs 1% pledge — flag before publishing.
- **chemical-pollution → reframe as waste/circularity** — DSNY monthly tonnage (borough-native,
  the best ecological dataset NYC publishes): diversion vs **Zero Waste 2030 (−90% vs 2005)**;
  organics capture ★ (curbside organics live). Disclose: commercial waste (~half the stream,
  Manhattan-concentrated) absent. Wedge is badly off-track — inherited framing.
- **(elevate) extreme heat as a named wedge** — HVI distribution (population-weighted, never a
  mean), surface temperature, ~489 heat-exacerbated deaths/yr, heat-stress deaths concentrated in
  Brooklyn/Bronx, zero at-home decedents had working AC ★ (his heat EO). The one ecological topic
  he has personally executive-ordered.
- **nitrogen-phosphorus-loading / ocean-acidification → harbor health** — Harbor Water Quality
  (station→waterbody crosswalk, documented judgment); CSO gallons vs 2030 LTCP; beach
  swimmability (Manhattan has no public beach — a finding, not a bug).

**Tier 2 (citywide only — show once, gray on borough portraits, never fake a split):**
- **climate-change (territorial)** — MOCEJ inventory vs **80x50 / net-zero (LL66)** and 57% of
  LL97 buildings over their 2030 caps ★ (enforcement KPI he owns). No borough field exists.
- **freshwater-withdrawals** — citywide ~115 GPD; impact is upstate watersheds; borough
  attribution conceptually wrong.
- **biodiversity-loss** — NAC/Parks ecological assessments; no Manhattan sample frame; narrative
  + gray wedge.
- **ozone-depletion** — does not exist at city scale anywhere; Amsterdam listed N/A. Narrative.

**Tier 3 (modeled, labeled as modeled, borough-aggregated):**
- **climate-change (consumption lens)** — MOCEJ/EcoDataLab 2019 CBEI is tract-level
  (18–58 tCO2e/household, ~3× spread) and city-endorsed: household-weighted borough aggregation is
  the only credible path. Benchmark: C40 1.5°C trajectory **5.9 tCO2e/capita by 2030**.
  Territorial ≈55 Mt vs consumption ≈92 Mt (+67%): territorial flatters NYC, consumption indicts
  it — **show both, label every wedge territorial-vs-consumption** (they rank boroughs in
  opposite orders: density favors Manhattan territorially; income-elastic consumption reverses it).

**Two methodology rules on the face of the portrait:** every ecological wedge carries its
territorial/consumption label; every ceiling states its sharing principle (equal per capita;
cite Häyhä 2016, Ryberg 2020, Hjalsted 2021; grandfathering rejected because it would treat
current overshoot as entitlement).

## 4. Lessons from precedents (what made portraits land or die)

- **Institutional owner + decision hook or it dies.** Portland/Philadelphia (workshops only) faded;
  Brussels (wired into subsidy strategy) and Cornwall (decision wheel on every Cabinet paper since
  2019) survived. Pitch a hook: portrait review in the Mayor's Management Report cycle, or a
  Cornwall-style screening wheel for Mayor's Office decisions.
- **Single legible numbers travel.** Amsterdam's overshoot ratios (climate 1.9×, land 2.6×);
  Leeds' "36 of 39 indicators outside safe/just." NYC's equivalents: "62% below the floor,"
  the 6.3-year borough life-expectancy gap, 57% of LL97 buildings over 2030 caps.
- **Pre-empt the greenwash critique** (Amsterdam's Party for the Animals problem) by naming
  contradictions on the face of the portrait: peaker plants, missed streets-mileage mandates
  (his own Streets Master Plan dropped LL195 benchmarks — a wedge would reimpose what he removed;
  flag before publishing), parks 0.5% vs 1%.
- **Publish the data-gap list** as a first-class deliverable ("portraits should create demand for
  missing metrics"). Honest gray beats tuned green — and several NYC gaps are actionable asks:
  borough GHG disaggregation, incarceration by borough of residence, shelter borough-of-origin,
  current borough life-expectancy publication (DOHMH stopped), commercial waste data.
- **Data Portrait vs Community Portrait** (DEAL's split): ship the Data Portrait now, name the
  Community Portrait (participatory aspiration-setting) as the follow-on — that's also the
  discussion-board feature of this product.
- DEAL's Indicator Library (2,000+ indicators from 30+ portraits) is the shortcut for any
  dimension where we're unsure.

## 5. Data gotchas for research sessions (bake into region config / prompts)

- NYPD shootings: use `5ucz-vwe8` (old `833y-fsy8` is archived).
- DOE absenteeism/graduation: InfoHub, not Open Data (frozen 2020-21).
- NYCgov poverty microdata on Open Data ends 2018 — use report tables (~2.5-yr lag).
- MMR moved to `/site/operations/reports/mmr.page`.
- nyc.gov and furmancenter.org 403 generic fetchers — evidence capture needs browser-like fetch.
- ACS 1-year publishes annually for all five boroughs (all are >65k counties); 5-yr for sub-borough.
- Publish a **reference year per indicator**, never one "data year" per doughnut (cadences range
  from monthly DSNY tonnage to triennial HVS to one-off 2019 CBEI).
