        02 / INTERPRET WITH CARE

        A useful claim keeps
        its conditions attached.

        This is a purposive, exploratory case study—not a systematic review, a city ranking, or an estimate of total Doughnut performance.

        01

        Separate observation
        from attribution

        Administrative counts and before–after changes describe what happened. Quasi-experimental estimates attempt to identify what the policy changed under explicit assumptions. Models and accounting have different meanings again.

        02

        Keep the denominator
        and geography

        A service-area demand reduction is not a citywide ecological score. Permits are not completed homes. A product emissions factor is not the same as real-world avoided emissions.

        03

        Keep inconvenient
        evidence in view

        Null, mixed, adverse, and contested findings remain visible. Costs and distribution matter. A plausible mechanism does not establish an impact on a Doughnut indicator or planetary boundary.
